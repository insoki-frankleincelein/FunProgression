Privacy

skiAudio · FunProgression · last updated 3 October 2026

FunProgression is an instrument. It runs on your device. Loops, chord choices, and the ringtone you render stay in storage on that device. They are not uploaded to an account, and there is no sign-in.

The app does not include ads, and it does not use analytics. It does not read your contacts, photos, or location.

If you paste a link to an audio file, the device fetches that file so it can hear the melody. The clip is not kept on a server. Streaming pages such as YouTube and Spotify are refused. Dropping a file never leaves the device.

The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts when the device is online. That request is between your device and Google.

Data retention: loops, chord choices, and rendered ringtones stay only in local storage on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall. Fetched audio clips used for melody extraction are processed on the device and are not retained on any skiAudio server. There is no account, so there is no cloud profile to delete; clearing app data or uninstalling removes what the app stored locally.
